The World

Not all signals resolve.

Driftwood Radio is a signal that carries what lingers. Cal builds fictional worlds to understand why nothing in her own world seems to stick — she doesn't know yet that's what she's doing. She just builds.

Each world transmits through the archive in two kinds of signals: Primary Transmissions — the version that fully lands — and Residual Transmissions — the ones that didn't fully resolve, but left an imprint.

World 1 is Brindleport — a small coastal harbor town. The kind of place people arrive at and stay longer than they meant to.

Getting to 10k

The human path to
10,000 listeners

No bots, no tricks — just consistent, authentic storytelling. TikTok drives to YouTube. The archive builds the audience. Here's the strategy that fits Driftwood Radio's world.

🎬
TikTok: Drive to YouTube
  • Open on a character moment — not a song intro. Show Ember's two mugs. Show the scarf on the floor. Hook before music.
  • First 3 seconds: character image + one line of dialogue on screen
  • Hook formula: "She keeps making two mugs. He's been gone three years. Ep. 3 — Ember's Morning."
  • Song plays underneath with lyrics as captions
  • End card always: "Full archive on YouTube → link in bio"
📸
Instagram: Character Portraits
  • Post each character image as its own portrait — caption written as their bio, not a description of them
  • "Ember. Innkeeper. Makes two mugs every morning. The second one is still Caulder's."
  • Reels: 60s clip with Brindleport color grade (warm amber, soft fog)
  • Stories polls: "Ember or Astrid?" / "Which song broke you?"
  • Highlights: The Cast · The Archive · Brindleport · Behind the Signal
▶️
YouTube: Three Playlists
  • World Order — episodes as the world was built
  • Timeline Order — story chronological order
  • Residual Archive — transmissions that didn't fully resolve
  • Thumbnail: Brindleport character + episode title + warm fog lighting
  • Pinned comment: link this page + "Start here → World Order playlist"
🔁
Consistency > Virality
  • Post on a schedule people can predict
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting
  • Ask questions: "Who do you think she's singing about?"
  • Reward followers who show up: call them out by name
  • Save fan comments as screenshots — share them as Stories
🎵
The Signal Finds Its Frequency
  • Upload each song to Suno — use the Brindleport aesthetic in covers (warm amber, fog, coastal stone)
  • Use TikTok Sounds — when your song becomes a sound, strangers build their own reels with it
  • Tags: #dreampop #sunoai #storysong #audiostory #brindleport
  • Cross-audience: #cottagecore #darkacademia #cozyfantasy #cozyreads — they love character-driven worlds
  • The Residual Transmissions (panic attack, frustration interludes) will land hardest — post those raw
